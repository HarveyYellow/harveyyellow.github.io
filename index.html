About Research Publications

About Me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia, advised by Prof. Ninghao Liu.

My current research focuses on model merging through on-policy distillation (OPD), with the goal of combining complementary capabilities from multiple models into a single model. I also explore computer-use agents (CUA).

Research Interests

  • Multi-model capability merging — combining complementary capabilities through OPD-based model merging.
  • Agentic CUA — agents that perceive, reason about, and interact with digital environments.
  • Model testing and evaluation — assessing capabilities, performance, reliability, and limitations.
