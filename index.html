Biography

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia, advised by Prof. Ninghao Liu. My current research focuses on model merging through on-policy distillation (OPD), with the goal of combining complementary capabilities from multiple models into a single model. I also explore computer-use agents (CUA).

Research Interests

  • Multi-model capability merging: combining complementary model capabilities, with a focus on OPD-based model merging.
  • Agentic CUA: computer-use agents that perceive, reason about, and interact with digital environments.
  • Model testing and evaluation: assessing model capabilities, performance, reliability, and limitations.
